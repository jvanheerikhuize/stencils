# Purpose

**Problem:** Creating 3D-printable stencils from vector artwork requires manual conversion steps, parameter tuning, and results are often fragile or misaligned for spray painting without detailed documentation.

**Audience:** Artists, hobbyists, and makers who want to convert artwork into physical stencils without learning CAD software or tedious manual conversion processes.

**Key constraints:** Must accept SVG input (standard vector format), auto-generate beveled edges and registration marks for spray alignment, produce ready-to-print STL files, and include design metadata.

**Success metric:** An artist uploads an SVG, gets a ready-to-print stencil STL with beveled edges and spray-paint alignment marks, and can immediately send to a 3D printer or request a physical print without additional design work.
